DESCRIPTION: Daridorexant population PK (Krause et al. 2023, final model): two compartments,
first-order absorption with lag time and dose-dependent bioavailability, Michaelis-Menten elimination.

[LONGITUDINAL]
input = {F, tlag, ka, Vc, Vp, Q, Vm, Km}

k12 = Q/Vc
